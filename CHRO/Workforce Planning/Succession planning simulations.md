### **Master Prompt: Succession Planning Simulation**

**Objective:**
Create a **realistic, data-driven succession planning simulation** for **[Organization Name/Industry]**. The simulation should identify, develop, and prepare internal talent for critical leadership roles, ensuring business continuity and minimizing disruption during transitions.

#### **1. Context & Scope**
- **Organization Profile:**
  - Industry: **[Industry]**
  - Size: **[Number of employees/Revenue]**
  - Current Leadership Structure: **[Hierarchy/Key Roles]**
  - Strategic Goals: **[Short-term and long-term objectives]**
- **Simulation Focus:**
  - Target Roles: **[List critical roles, e.g., CEO, CFO, Department Heads]**
  - Time Horizon: **[Short-term: 1-2 years / Long-term: 3-5 years]**
  - Key Challenges: **[e.g., Aging workforce, skill gaps, high turnover, industry disruption]**

#### **2. Data Inputs**
Provide the following data to inform the simulation:
- **Workforce Demographics:**
  - Age distribution, tenure, performance ratings, potential ratings (9-box grid if available).
- **Competency Framework:**
  - **Leadership competencies** required for target roles (e.g., strategic thinking, emotional intelligence, change management).
- **Current Talent Pool:**
  - High-potential employees (HiPos), their strengths, development areas, and readiness levels.
- **Historical Data:**
  - Past succession events, internal vs. external hires, and their success rates.
- **External Benchmarks:**
  - Industry standards for succession planning (e.g., average time to fill critical roles, common development programs).

#### **3. Simulation Design**
- **Scenario Parameters:**
  - **Base Case:** "Business as usual" with no major disruptions.
  - **Stress Scenarios:**
    - Sudden departure of a key leader (e.g., CEO resigns).
    - Market downturn requiring cost-cutting and restructuring.
    - Rapid growth requiring scaling leadership quickly.
    - Mergers/Acquisitions integrating new leadership teams.
- **Variables to Test:**
  - Impact of **internal vs. external hiring** on culture and performance.
  - Effectiveness of **development programs** (mentoring, job rotation, training).
  - **Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI)** outcomes in succession decisions.
  - **Cost implications** of different succession strategies.

#### **4. Output Requirements**
Generate the following deliverables from the simulation:
- **Succession Pipeline Visualization:**
  - A **9-box grid** or **talent map** showing readiness vs. potential for each critical role.
  - **Risk assessment**: Roles with no ready successors ("bench strength gaps").
- **Development Plans:**
  - Customized **Individual Development Plans (IDPs)** for HiPos, including:
    - Stretch assignments, mentoring, training, and timelines.
- **Scenario Analysis Report:**
  - **Pros/cons** of each scenario (e.g., internal promotion vs. external hire).
  - **Financial impact** (e.g., cost of hiring externally vs. developing internally).
  - **Cultural impact** (e.g., morale, retention, DEI).
- **Actionable Recommendations:**
  - **Immediate steps**: Quick wins to address critical gaps.
  - **Long-term strategies**: Building a sustainable pipeline (e.g., leadership academies, cross-training).
  - **Contingency plans**: "Break glass" options for emergency successions.

#### **5. Evaluation Metrics**
Define how success will be measured:
- **Short-term:**
  - % of critical roles with identified successors.
  - Reduction in time-to-fill for key positions.
  - Employee engagement scores related to career development.
- **Long-term:**
  - Success rate of internal promotions (retention after 12/24 months).
  - Diversity metrics in leadership roles.
  - Business performance during transitions (e.g., revenue stability, customer satisfaction).

#### **6. Tools & Methodologies**
- **Analytical Tools:**
  - Use **predictive analytics** to forecast attrition and readiness.
  - Apply **Monte Carlo simulations** for probabilistic outcomes.
- **Frameworks:**
  - **9-Box Grid** for talent assessment.
  - **SWOT Analysis** for scenario planning.
  - **Balanced Scorecard** to align succession with strategic goals.

#### **7. Stakeholder Engagement**
- **Key Stakeholders:**
  - **HR Leadership**, **Current Executives**, **Board of Directors**, **High-Potential Employees**.
- **Communication Plan:**
  - How to **transparently communicate** the process to avoid speculation or anxiety.
  - **Feedback loops** to refine the simulation based on stakeholder input.

#### **8. Assumptions & Constraints**
- **Assumptions:**
  - Employees are open to relocation if required.
  - Budget for development programs is **[X]%** of HR budget.
- **Constraints:**
  - Legal/regulatory requirements (e.g., labor laws, DEI compliance).
  - Union agreements (if applicable).

#### **9. Example Output Format**
Provide the simulation results in the following structure:
1. **Executive Summary** (1-page overview of findings).
2. **Talent Pipeline Dashboard** (visualization of readiness).
3. **Scenario Deep Dives** (detailed analysis of each scenario).
4. **Development Roadmap** (timeline for HiPo programs).
5. **Risk Mitigation Plan** (contingencies for high-risk roles).

#### **10. Customization Instructions**
- Replace **placeholders** (in **bold**) with organization-specific data.
- Adjust **scenarios** to reflect industry-specific risks (e.g., tech disruption, regulatory changes).
- Tailor **competencies** to align with the organization’s leadership model.

### **Final Notes**
- **Tone:** Professional, data-driven, but accessible to non-HR stakeholders.
- **Audience:** HR leaders, executives, and board members.
- **Flexibility:** Design the simulation to be **iterative**—allow for updates as the organization evolves.
